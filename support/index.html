SudoWise 1.0 Support

Need help?

Contact Support

For questions, feedback, bug reports, or subscription support, email support@gamacoraggio.com.

When reporting a problem, include the screen or feature, what you expected, what happened, and your iPhone/iOS version when possible.

SudoWise Coach

Coach progresses from direction to technique, focus, explanation, and finally a verified move. Free and Pro access may differ by feature.

Technique Map

Technique lessons explain how to recognize and apply supported solving strategies in a progressive sequence.

Practice & Mastery

Practice recommendations use your current mastery, confidence, prerequisites, recent performance, and readiness for the next technique.

Sound & Haptics

Sound and haptic feedback can be enabled or disabled independently in Settings.

Subscriptions & Restore Purchases

SudoWise Pro is offered through Apple’s App Store as monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions.

If an active subscription is not recognized, open the Pro screen and use Restore Purchases. Use Manage Subscription to open Apple’s subscription controls.

Apple Intelligence

AI-enhanced wording requires a supported device, SudoWise Pro, Apple Intelligence availability, and the related SudoWise setting enabled.

If unavailable, SudoWise automatically falls back to deterministic coaching and recommendation explanations.

Quick Troubleshooting

  1. Confirm your selected puzzle size and challenge preference.
  2. Close and reopen SudoWise if a screen does not refresh as expected.
  3. For Pro-only features, verify the Pro screen shows an active entitlement.
  4. For Apple Intelligence features, confirm Apple Intelligence is enabled and available on the device.
  5. If a prior purchase is missing, use Restore Purchases.
  6. If the issue persists, contact support with the details above.
