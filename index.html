A smarter way to learn Sudoku.

SudoWise is a Sudoku coach built to help you understand how to solve, not just which number goes next. Learn with progressive coaching, build technique mastery, and practice what matters most.

Progressive SudoWise Coach

Move from direction, to technique, to focus, explanation, and finally the verified move only when you need it.

Technique Map

Build skills from Naked and Hidden Singles through pairs, triples, pointing techniques, Box-Line Reduction, and X-Wing.

Personalized Practice

Practice recommendations adapt to your mastery, confidence, recent solving, prerequisites, and readiness for what comes next.

Track Real Mastery

See what is developing, what is strong, what needs reinforcement, and which techniques are ready to unlock next.

Play your way

Solve 4×4, 6×6, and classic 9×9 Sudoku puzzles across multiple difficulty levels. SudoWise combines deterministic puzzle logic with an educational interface designed for learning, practice, and mastery.

SudoWise Pro

SudoWise Pro unlocks unlimited puzzles, personalized practice plans, the full Technique Map and lessons, unrestricted coaching, and detailed mastery insights.

Monthly and annual subscriptions are available through Apple’s App Store. There is no free trial at launch.

Private by design

SudoWise does not require an account. Detailed puzzle history, mastery, progress, and preferences are designed to remain on your device by default. Optional privacy-friendly analytics help improve the app without sending puzzle contents, individual moves, or detailed learning history.
