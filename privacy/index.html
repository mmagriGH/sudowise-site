Privacy Policy

Local-first by design.

SudoWise does not require an account or login. Detailed Sudoku learning data is designed to remain on your device by default.

Local Learning Data

SudoWise stores puzzle attempts, history, mastery, confidence, practice state, preferences, settings, and related learning data locally on your device.

SudoWise does not upload puzzle contents, individual cell moves, candidates, notes, or detailed learning history as product analytics.

Product Analytics (TelemetryDeck)

SudoWise uses TelemetryDeck to understand broad product usage, puzzle engagement, coaching and learning feature adoption, app performance, and subscription conversion. In the current release, product analytics is enabled by default for new installations. You can disable or re-enable analytics at any time in More → Data & Privacy → Product Analytics.

When enabled, event data can include actions such as app launches, puzzle starts and completions, feature navigation, coaching interactions, broad difficulty and grid-size categories, grouped solve-duration and hint-use ranges, and subscription-screen and purchase-flow outcomes. SudoWise does not intentionally send your first name, puzzle grid contents, individual cell moves, notes, candidates, puzzle seeds, detailed history, or detailed technique-mastery records as event properties.

TelemetryDeck receives an anonymized installation identifier, event names, approximate event times, app and device metadata, and permitted broad event properties. This information is used for product analytics, not advertising or cross-app tracking.

Disabling Product Analytics stops future app-initiated usage events. Previously received anonymized event records are not automatically deleted and may remain in TelemetryDeck storage, including archived storage, subject to its published retention practices. Because the data is anonymized, deletion of individual historical records may not be available. See TelemetryDeck's privacy FAQ or contact us with questions.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, SudoWise Pro may use Apple Intelligence to improve the wording of verified coaching explanations and recommendation rationale. Sudoku logic, puzzle generation, solutions, hints, difficulty, recommendations, and mastery remain deterministic and are not delegated to generative AI.

If Apple Intelligence is unavailable or disabled, SudoWise falls back to deterministic explanations.

Subscriptions and Purchases

SudoWise Pro subscriptions, purchases, restores, entitlement status, and subscription management are handled through Apple StoreKit and App Store services. SudoWise receives the product and entitlement information necessary to determine Pro access. SudoWise does not receive your payment-card details.

Advertising, Tracking, and Data Sales

SudoWise does not include advertising networks, does not use app data for cross-app advertising tracking, and does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Deletion

Removing SudoWise from your device may remove locally stored puzzle and learning data, subject to normal iOS device and backup behavior. App Store purchase history is managed by Apple.

Removing local app data does not erase anonymized TelemetryDeck analytics already transmitted. You can disable future analytics under More → Data & Privacy. For access, deletion, or privacy questions, contact support@gamacoraggio.com.

Children’s Privacy

SudoWise does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as SudoWise changes. Material changes will be reflected in the published policy and, when appropriate, in the app.
